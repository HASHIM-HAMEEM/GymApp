Apex · Membership System
Design System Member App Admin Flows
Design System · V2.0 · September 2026

Dark, quiet, and certain. Membership at reception speed.

Version two keeps the calm and trades ivory for ink. Apex is a boutique gym membership product for two roles — members and reception staff. The surface is strictly monochrome: near-black grounds, warm-free grays, and a single white accent that means touch. Status colors stay strictly semantic — desaturated, never neon. Space Grotesk handles display and numerals, JetBrains Mono handles identity — IDs, timestamps, anything that must never wobble.

01 — Principles

How the product behaves

Six commitments. Every screen decision traces back to one of them — the difference between a members' club and a dashboard.
P1
Status at a glance

Validity is answered from Home — a ring, a number, a color. Nothing important waits behind a tap.

P2
The QR is one tap away

A raised white button lives in the center of the dock. From anywhere, the member card is a single reach of the thumb.

P3
One accent, honestly used

White marks interactive intent and brand — nothing else competes. Green, amber, and red never appear except to tell membership truth.

P4
Reception-speed UI

The scanner lives in near-black. Verdicts are full-screen, one-word readable at arm's length, with the member's name set in mono.

P5
Consequence asks first

Renewals and publishes always pass through a review step. Short actions get sheets; important work gets full pages.

P6
Localize by design

Arabic is a first-class layout: IBM Plex Sans Arabic, mirrored grids, and room for names twice as long.
