Role 01 — Member · iOS · 390 × 844

The member app

A member opens the app for three reasons: to see that their membership is fine, to walk past reception, and to hear from the club. Every screen below answers one of those without detours. Status is visible from Home; the QR card is one tap from anywhere; expired and due states never borrow the active look.

Entry

Arrival & sign-in

Phone-number sign-in with a six-digit code. No passwords to forget at the front desk.
Home

Status without opening anything

The ring answers "am I fine?" in under a second. Everything else is context.
Access

The card at reception

One tap on the raised dock button. Screen brightness is the phone's job; contrast is ours — the QR always sits on a light plate.
Membership

Every state, honestly drawn

Six states of one screen. Layout never changes; only the truth does — color, ring, and the primary action.
Visits

A quiet record

Check-ins as a ledger, not a feed. No streaks, no graphs, no judgment.
Notices

From the club, in the club's voice

Announcements with category, date, and read state. No chat, no comments, no likes.
Profile

Details and editing

Read-only facts live in rows; editing is a separate screen with real validation.
